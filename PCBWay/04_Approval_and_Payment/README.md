Save approved quote and receipt in Receipts_and_Approvals. Don't store passwords, card details or identity documents in this Git project. Supplier responses/receipts are excluded from Git by the local .gitignore rules; do not force-add sensitive documents.

Case fit and powered behavior remain prototype qualification work. If the power process is too expensive, stop and consider a separately reviewed revision; do not silently cheapen the via process or swap U13.
