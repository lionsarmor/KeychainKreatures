# Upload power P.4 Gerbers

Upload **UPLOAD_POWER_P4_GERBERS.zip** to the separate POWER PCB + ASSEMBLY job.

Request five finished assemblies, 50 × 50 mm, 1.6 mm, **4 copper layers** in order F.Cu / In1.Cu / In2.Cu / B.Cu. Follow FABRICATION_REQUIREMENTS.md and VIA_IN_PAD_REVIEW.csv.

Hold fabrication pending approval of filled/capped/planarized component-pad vias, fine-pitch finish/stencil and WCSP assembly. Do not replace this requirement with ordinary soldermask tenting. Stencil thickness and panel tooling must be proposed by the assembler, not guessed.
