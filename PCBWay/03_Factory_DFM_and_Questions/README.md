# Manufacturing review — approval required

Give PCBWay the fabrication requirements and via-in-pad list here. Use DFM_RESPONSE_CHECKLIST.csv to track written answers; every item starts PENDING. Save their returned PDFs, CAM images and emails under Supplier_Responses.

Required subjects include layer count/stackup, copper and drills, filled/capped/planarized component-pad vias, U13 WCSP and fine-pitch packages, finish/stencil, actual BOM manufacturers and substitutions, centroid convention, connector operations, electrical test, hidden-joint inspection and cost.

Ordinary tenting is not equivalent to filled/capped via-in-pad processing. [PCBWay describes the resin-fill/electroplated-cap approach](https://www.pcbway.com/helpcenter/ordering_parameter_instruction/Via_in_Pad.html). Capability in general does not constitute approval of these boards.

Engineering acceptance of manufacturability does not certify circuit function, battery safety or the finished toy. Do not treat the saved zero-violation CAD reports as measured functional tests.
