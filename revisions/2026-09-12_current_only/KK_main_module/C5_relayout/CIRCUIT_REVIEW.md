# C.5 main-board circuit review

## Verdict and limits

This is a **static engineering review, not a powered test or a guarantee of operation**. The placement ECO preserves all 98 component values and all 297 component pad/net assignments. The main-board topology is coherent under the supply, module and firmware assumptions below. ERC alone does not verify those assumptions. Final routing results are reported separately in `DRC.json`, `VERIFICATION.json` and `ROUTE_AUDIT.json` when completed.

**Do not plug this main board into an arbitrary supply.** J1 still requires **1=regulated 5 V, 2=GND, 3=regulated 3.3 V, 4=regulated 3.2 V**, supplied and sequenced together. It is not a battery connector and not a single-rail input. The power module remains a separate unfinished project. USB and J1 must not be powered together until source isolation/backfeed behavior is qualified. This pass does not add a charger, battery protection, regulator or USB power mux.

## What was checked

| Block | Static finding | Required hardware/firmware check |
|---|---|---|
| ESP32-S3 SuperMini | GPIO3 strap pin left unused; native USB GPIO19/20 are not repurposed on the carrier. GPIO1/2 handle interrupt/function, 4/5 I²C, 6/7 IR, 8 audio PWM, 9 backlight, 10 SD select, 11/12/13 SPI. TX/RX are used for TFT DC/CS, so they are not spare UART pins. Module 3V3 output is **not** tied to the external logic rail. | Verify the delivered module revision, its GPIO labels, 5 V input path, regulator and actual flash/PSRAM. Do not promise a memory size from the SuperMini name. Its USB is a service/programming connection on the rear; shell removal may be needed. |
| MCP23017 U1 | 3.3 V supply, grounded address pins, reset pull-up, button pull-ups and active-low button wiring retained. U1 drives amplifier enable, motor enable, TFT reset and RGB controls. | Configure output latches to safe values before changing pin direction; mirror both interrupt banks onto INTA if all eight expander buttons must wake/report through the single interrupt wire. Debounce switches. Do not initialize RGB or actuators with uncontrolled outputs. |
| Screen J2 | Existing 8-pin interface, 3.3 V logic and separate backlight driver retained. Physical rotation changes neither pin order nor net function. Controller supports rotation; use a 280 × 240 landscape logical viewport for the existing 240 × 280 panel. | Verify delivered header order, ST7789 panel offsets/inversion and rotation with a test pattern. Check actual BLK circuitry before reducing the conservative 100 Ω R23. Screen rotation is a firmware setting as well as a PCB change. |
| SD J3 | 3.3 V-only breakout: 1=3V3, 2=CS, 3=MOSI, 4=CLK, 5=MISO, 6=GND. Screen and SD share SCK/MOSI but have separate selects. Only the SD uses MISO. | Verify idle-select levels, bus sharing and SPI frequencies on actual modules. Start slowly. Use safe write/rename/update handling; a permanently installed card is still vulnerable to power-loss corruption. Never feed this module 5 V. |
| IR transmitter D1/Q1 | TSAL6200 polarity retained, Q1 E/B/C mapping checked, 100 Ω LED resistor R24, 1 kΩ base resistor R25, 100 kΩ default-off R26. With illustrative VF=1.35 V and saturated VCE=0.2 V, current is about (3.3−1.35−0.2)/100 = **17.5 mA while on**. Base-drive estimate is about 2.5 mA at VBE=0.8 V. This is a modest-range emitter, not a high-current blaster. | Drive modulated bursts, not DC. Test protocol/range and supply noise. Proposed top-facing lead form must be made without stressing the epoxy; confirm polarity before soldering. |
| IR receiver U2 | **TSOP38238 is fitted**, pin 1 output, 2 ground, 3 filtered supply. R27/C14/C15 filter retained, active-low output goes to GPIO6. Receiver and emitter both aim toward the top edge from the rear. | This is a **38 kHz demodulating remote-control receiver**, not a universal raw IR waveform capture front end. Use supported carrier/burst timing. Test ambient light and self-blinding; provide clear optical windows and preferably a separating baffle. |
| RGB D3/U4 | Common anode uses 5 V; TLC5916 logic uses 3.3 V. R40=1.8 kΩ gives approximately 10.4 mA/channel at the default gain. OE is pulled high and data/clock have default-state resistors. Unused outputs are unused, not shorted. Pin order remains 1 red cathode, 2 common anode, 3 blue cathode, 4 green cathode. | Latch zero before enabling outputs. TLC5916 gives per-channel on/off and global current adjustment, **not independent hardware PWM for each color**. Smooth independent fades would need software timing or a future driver change. Actual bare-LED lead retention in the four-pin socket is unqualified. |
| Audio U3 | UTC TDA2822 **DIP-8** bridge topology retained: outputs 1 and 3 to J5, supply 2, ground 4, input 7, second input 6 grounded, feedback 8/5. Feedback, bypass and output stability networks are clustered. No speaker terminal is ground. | Verify the exact amplifier variant/pinout and speaker power rating. Scope speaker voltage differentially; never attach a grounded scope clip to either speaker output. Test clipping, oscillation, PWM residue, DC, pops and temperature at low initial volume. No transistor-level audio simulation was performed. |
| Amplifier power gate Q5/Q6 | Retains the C.2 TN0702 NMOS/LP0701 PMOS change. Both footprints map 1 source, 2 gate, 3 drain. R37 holds Q5 off; R39 holds Q6 off. Calculated enabled gate-control current remains about **64 µA**, excluding amplifier current. The PMOS is resistive: its specified 2 Ω maximum at −3 V/150 mA/25°C corresponds to 0.30 V drop and 45 mW at that test point. | Measure amplifier rail headroom, turn-on inrush and temperature. Do not fit the obsolete bipolar transistors at Q5/Q6. The low-voltage source and gate-drive margin require coordinated supply qualification. |
| Backlight Q2/Q3 | Existing NPN/PNP high-side driver and default-off bias retained. Its bipolar drive consumes current while enabled; unlike the amplifier switch, it was not changed to MOSFETs in this ECO. With illustrative junction drops, the 220 Ω base-path resistor can draw roughly **11 mA** while enabled. | Measure actual display load and transistor drive before brightness or battery-life claims. The circuit is not optimized for minimum sleep/active current; an optional MOSFET backlight ECO should follow module qualification rather than guessing BLK load. |
| Motor Q4/D2/J4 | TN0702 S/G/D mapping and 1N5819 flyback orientation checked: diode cathode to actuator supply, anode to switched motor return. C16 bridges the motor connector; C17 is the nearby actuator reservoir. Gate default-off pull-down retained. | Verify starting/stall current, hot/cold margin and interference. TN0702 is not zero resistance; motor startup depends on the 3.2 V source. Add suppression at motor terminals if long harnesses need it. |
| Resistors/capacitors | Values/MPNs retained; 25 low-current pull-up/bias resistors now use upright 2.54 mm lead forms. Audio network and load resistors remain flat. Electrolytic polarity is retained. | Form leads with a jig and inspect for exposed-lead shorts. Nominal 3D packages are not maximum-tolerance supplier models. Keep probe holes bare beneath other-side assemblies. |

## Routing policy and mechanical checks

The copied project unexpectedly contained only a 0.2 mm default netclass. C.5 explicitly restores **0.25 mm signals, 0.30 mm audio, 0.50 mm speaker/backlight/IR, 0.60 mm explicit ground and 0.80 mm power/motor**, with 0.20 mm clearance. Native DSN output was inspected to verify these widths actually reached the router. No DRC severity was relaxed to make this pass.

The 84 × 95 mm outline is 13.26% less area than 80 × 115 mm C.4. Four rounded corners, mounting holes, 25 bare debug pads and both ground pours are retained. Rear footprints were packed conservatively against front component courtyards, not just same-side bodies. The elevated display can cover trimmed rear-component solder tails, subject to measured standoff and insulation.

The ESP32's rear-side flip was checked in the 3D viewer: **antenna at top, USB toward the center**. Copper keep-outs include the edge band and the area under the antenna, between the pin rows. Keep screen metal, battery, screws and harnesses out of that region too. RF performance remains a sample test.

**Smaller area does not mean a thin enclosure.** Existing socket stacks are tall: the proposed RGB reaches about 22.1 mm above the front surface, and the MCU model about 15.24 mm above the rear surface. A flat envelope enclosing both faces therefore needs roughly **39 mm component-to-component depth**, before case walls and clearance. A local RGB bump/light-pipe arrangement or a qualified lower socket could change that later; neither is silently assumed here. Support the single-edge screen socket with shell features/spacers, and leave SD eject and JST latch/wire clearance. No battery position or capacity has been invented.

## Bring-up gates before a student/product batch

1. Dry-fit real modules, sockets, upright resistors, RGB and formed IR LED against the **C.5** fit sheets. Verify front/rear lead tails, retention, optical direction and screen support.
2. Verify supply pins and polarity with an unpowered meter. Use a current-limited, coordinated three-rail bench source or the qualified future power module; never a raw cell at J1.
3. Test MCU boot and safe output states, then buttons/expander, test-pattern display, SD read/write and RGB separately.
4. Bring up IR, motor and low-volume audio independently while measuring rail dips and current.
5. Test simultaneous Wi-Fi + display + SD write + motor + audio; then check power interruption/recovery, thermals, RF/IR range and handling.
6. Only after those gates, freeze shell/battery choices, volume/brightness limits and kit-production instructions.
